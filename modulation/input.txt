One sunny morning, a little rabbit went into the forest to pick carrots.
Suddenly, he heard a small bird crying. It fell down from the tree and could not fly back home.
The rabbit was kind and brave. He climbed the tall tree slowly and helped the bird return to its nest.
The bird’s mother was very thankful. She smiled and said, “Kindness makes you the bravest of all!”
From that day on, the little rabbit always tried to help others.
