One warm and sunny spring morning, a fluffy little rabbit named Lulu hopped happily into the green forest. His soft white fur glowed in the sunlight, and his long ears twitched gently as he walked. He planned to pick fresh, sweet carrots and juicy wild grasses for his family's dinner.
The forest was quiet and lively. Little butterflies danced among colorful flowers, and gentle wind brushed through the leaves. Lulu was enjoying his walk, until he heard a soft, sad crying sound not far away.
He stopped hopping and listened carefully. The cry came from an old oak tree. He ran over quickly and found a tiny baby bird lying on the soft grass. Its small wings were shaking weakly, and it could not fly back to its high nest. It had accidentally fallen down while learning to fly.
